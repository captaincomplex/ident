Before you write

These three cover the majority of messages.

Wrong date on screen

The Pi has no battery clock. If it loses Wi-Fi it freezes on the last date it knew. Reconnect it and the clock fixes itself in a minute or two.

Won't join Wi-Fi

On a Pi Zero 2 W the network must be 2.4GHz — it cannot see 5GHz at all. Check that before anything else.

Roster not appearing

Check the calendar link is the full .ics URL and hasn't expired. Failing that, upload your roster PDF instead.
